Privacy Policy

Small Start — published by Firefly Software.
Last updated: 25 November 2026.

The short version. Small Start has no accounts and no sign-up. Your routines, steps, schedules, streaks and history are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to us. We show no ads and we do not track you across other apps or websites. Two kinds of information leave your device: what our payments provider needs to confirm your purchase, and anonymous usage statistics that tell us which parts of the app are used. Neither contains anything you type into the app.

1. Who we are

Small Start is developed and published by Firefly Software ("we", "us"). You can reach us at fireflysoftware1@gmail.com for any question about this policy or your data.

2. Information stored on your device

The app creates and stores the following locally, in its own private app storage. We cannot read it, and it is never transmitted to us:

  • Routines you create or install from a template, their steps, durations, emoji and weekday schedules
  • Reminder times and whether reminders are enabled
  • Your completion history and streaks
  • A record of which reminders were delivered and whether you opened them — used only to vary reminder wording and to notice when a reminder time isn't working for you
  • App preferences: light/dark theme choice, whether you have completed onboarding, and the date your free trial started

The trial start date is additionally stored in the operating system's secure keychain so that reinstalling the app does not restart the trial. It contains a date only — no personal information.

3. Information that leaves your device

The app communicates with three services: RevenueCat for purchases, Google Firebase Analytics for usage statistics, and a usage statistics service that we run ourselves on Cloudflare.

Purchases (RevenueCat)

Small Start uses RevenueCat to process and validate in-app purchases and subscriptions. When you make a purchase, restore a purchase, or when the app checks whether your purchase is still active, RevenueCat receives:

DataWhy
Purchase and subscription status (which product, whether it is active, renewal and expiry dates) To unlock the paid version and to restore it on a new device
An anonymous identifier generated by RevenueCat To link a purchase to the same installation. It is not your name, email or Apple ID, and we cannot use it to identify you
Basic technical information about the app and device required by the purchase SDK (such as app version and platform) To operate the purchase system
Anonymous product counters, such as how many times the paid options screen was opened, how many routines have been created and how many routine runs have been completed To understand, in aggregate, whether the paid version is useful. These are counts only. They contain no routine names, step names, times or any other content you enter

Payments themselves are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see or receive your card details, billing address or Apple/Google account credentials.

Usage statistics (Google Firebase Analytics)

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to count how the app is used. Firebase receives:

  • Usage events, such as the app being opened, a screen being shown, a routine being created or a run being finished
  • An app-instance identifier that Firebase creates for this installation. It is not your name, email or Apple/Google account
  • General device information, such as device model, operating system version, language and country

Advertising features are switched off. The app does not collect an advertising identifier and this data is not used for advertising.

Usage statistics (our own service)

The app also sends usage statistics to a service operated by Firefly Software and hosted on Cloudflare. We use it to see which screens are opened, where people get stuck and where they stop. It receives:

  • The names of the screens you open and how many seconds you stay on each
  • Events such as onboarding finished, a run started, a run left before the end or a save that was refused, with small counts and yes/no values
  • The app version, the platform (iOS or Android), the major operating system version and a two-letter language code
  • The week in which the app was installed and how many days ago that was, up to 90

It does not receive a user identifier, an installation identifier or a device identifier. Each time the app is opened it creates a new random session number, so two sessions cannot be linked to each other. The service does not store your IP address.

None of these statistics contain routine names, step names, times of day, dates or any other text you enter.

Versions before 1.4.0 had a switch in Settings to turn usage statistics off. If you turned it off, it stays off.

4. What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or account of any kind
  • No location data
  • No contacts, photos, calendar or health data
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA) — the app contains no advertising SDK
  • No analytics that identify you — usage statistics carry no name, email or account, and nothing you type into the app
  • The content of your routines and steps is never transmitted anywhere

5. Tracking and advertising

Small Start does not display advertising and does not track you across other apps or websites. We do not sell or share your information with data brokers. The app does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission because it does not track.

6. Notifications

Reminders are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device by the operating system. No reminder content is sent through a server, and we do not use push notifications. You can turn reminders off per routine inside the app, or revoke notification permission in your device settings at any time.

7. Children

Small Start is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. The app requires no account and collects no personal information from any user.

8. How long data is kept

Data stored on your device remains until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes your routines and history permanently; we hold no copy and cannot restore it.

Purchase records held by RevenueCat and by Apple or Google are retained according to their own policies and for as long as required for billing, tax and subscription management.

Usage statistics sent to our own service are kept as individual events for 30 days. After that only daily totals remain, such as how many sessions opened a screen. Data held by Google Firebase Analytics is retained according to Google's policies.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete personal information we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. Because we hold no account and no directly identifying information, most of your data is under your own control on your device.

Usage statistics sent to our own service carry no identifier, so we cannot find, export or delete the events of one person. If you have a question about them, write to us.

For anything relating to purchase records, write to fireflysoftware1@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. To help us locate the record, include the approximate purchase date and the store you purchased from.
